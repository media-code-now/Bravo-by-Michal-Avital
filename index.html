Our story

“Fashion is what you buy, style is what you do with it.”

Bravo Malibu began in 2015 when Michal Avital started importing jewelry from Israel. It grew into a curated blend of bohemian chic, urban flair and laid-back California style, now one of the best-loved boutiques in Greater Los Angeles and Malibu.

Our mission is to pair the sophistication of high-end Italian fashion with the easy charm of coastal living, at prices that stay within reach.

Michal Avital, founder
