The founder

“I never wanted to sell clothes. I wanted to help women walk out feeling like the best version of themselves.”

Bravo Malibu began in 2015 when Michal Avital started importing jewelry from Israel to a tiny storefront by the beach. Clients kept asking what she was wearing with it, so she started sourcing that too: a blouse from Florence, denim from Veneto, a bag that would become famous.

Today the boutique sits on Beverly Drive and the mission hasn't changed. Pair the sophistication of high-end Italian fashion with the easy charm of coastal living, at prices that stay within reach, and never let a client leave without a complete look.

What we stand for

Italian craft

We work directly with family ateliers in Florence, Milan and Veneto. Small runs, natural fibres, hand finishing.

Coastal ease

Everything has to work in real life: sand, car seats, a long dinner. If it doesn't move with you, we don't carry it.

Styling first

Nobody at Bravo is on commission. Our job is to send you home with outfits, not items, whether online or in store.
