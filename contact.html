Good to know

Frequently asked

Shipping, returns, sizing and gifting, answered. Still stuck? Talk to a stylist.

How fast is shipping?

Orders placed before 2pm PT ship the same business day from Beverly Hills. US delivery is 2–4 days; complimentary over $250, otherwise $12 flat. International rates are shown at checkout.

What's your return policy?

Return within 14 days of delivery for a full refund, or exchange in store any time. Items should be unworn with tags attached. Returns are free; just use the prepaid label in your parcel.

How does Italian sizing run?

We convert every piece to US sizing and note the fit on each product page. Relaxed pieces: take your usual size. Fitted pieces: size up if between. Or text us your measurements and we'll tell you exactly.

Can I pick up in store?

Yes. Choose "Pickup on Beverly Drive" at checkout and we'll have it ready within two hours during opening times. Try it on while you're here and swap sizes on the spot.

Do you offer gift wrapping and gift cards?

Complimentary gift wrapping on every order; add a note at checkout. Digital gift cards are available from $50 and arrive by email instantly.

Is a styling session really free?

Completely. Nobody at Bravo is on commission. Book a time, tell us the occasion, and come in curious.
